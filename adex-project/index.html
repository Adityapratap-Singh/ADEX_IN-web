PRODUCT 01 · IN DEVELOPMENT

ADEX_ISL

Technology for Indian Sign Language

The idea

ADEX_ISL explores how computer vision and local AI can recognize and interpret Indian Sign Language in a practical, accessible way.

The opportunity

Many people who use sign language still face communication barriers with people who do not. This product direction is focused on making everyday interaction more inclusive.

What we are exploring

  • Indian Sign Language gesture recognition
  • Real-time translation support
  • Privacy-conscious local processing
  • Accessible experiences for real-world use

Our approach

We are starting with real communication needs, testing thoughtfully, and improving the experience with the people it is designed to serve.
